A better trip.

Support for Tabi, the Japan itinerary planner for iPhone and iPad. Pick your dates, preview your route, and keep your plans with you offline.

Plan your first trip

  1. Tap Choose my dates.
  2. Select your first and last day in Japan. Both dates count; choose the same date for a one-day visit. Trips can span 1–30 days.
  3. Tap Suggest my itinerary to preview each day.
  4. Tap Save my trip. Pace, interests, and cities are optional under Make it yours.

Plans are suggestions, not bookings. Check current opening hours, tickets, seasonal closures, and transport before you go.

Common questions

Does Tabi work offline?

Your saved itinerary, curated place information, phrasebook text, and travel tools are stored on your device. Maps and external directions may require a connection or separately downloaded maps. Spoken audio depends on the voices installed on your device.

Do I need an account or subscription?

No. Tabi does not require an account, subscription, or in-app purchase. Itinerary suggestions are calculated on your device.

Can I change the suggestion?

Before saving, use Change dates or preferences. After saving, open a day to adjust your plans and notes. You can also start a new trip from the Trip tab. Export a backup first if you want to keep the old trip.

How do I back up or move my trip?

Open Settings in Tabi and export a backup. On the other device, open Settings and restore it. A backup can contain your traveler details, notes, and health information; keep it private. Restoring replaces the app data on the destination device.

The app is not saving. What can I do?

Keep Tabi open, check available device storage, and export a backup if possible. Avoid deleting the app before saving a backup. If the problem persists, report it using the support link below.

How do I erase my data?

Use Settings → Erase all my data. This removes your saved trip, personal entries, and preferences from this device. Exported copies and device backups must be managed separately.

Contact support

Report a problem or request a feature in the public Tabi support repository. Include your device model, iOS version, Tabi version, and the steps that caused the problem.

GitHub issues are public. Do not include passwords, booking references, your backup file, private travel details, health information, email addresses, or phone numbers.

Open a support request

A GitHub account is needed to open an issue. For privacy concerns, describe the issue without including your personal data.
