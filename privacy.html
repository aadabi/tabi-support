Privacy policy

Effective October 21, 2026 · Applies to the Tabi mobile app provided by AI 2 X Co.

Tabi does not require an account. Your itinerary is planned on your device, and the app does not include advertising or analytics tracking.

Information stored on your device

Tabi stores your itinerary, traveler names, hotels, flights, notes, expenses, preferences, and any health information you choose to enter locally on your device. Entering personal or health details is optional. We do not operate a Tabi server that receives these entries or your itinerary-generation requests.

Permissions and external services

Location access is optional for map features and is controlled in your device settings. Maps, external directions, phone calls, and links use Apple or other providers you choose, subject to their privacy policies. Those providers may process information needed for their services, such as a destination or map location. These services may require internet access.

The current trip-planning flow does not request photo-library or microphone access. System features, installed voices, and device-level permissions are controlled by your operating system.

Backups and sharing

If you choose to export or share a backup, it can contain all the saved app information, including personal travel and health details. You choose where to send or store that file. Tabi does not upload it to us automatically. Your operating system may also include app data in device backups according to your system settings.

Retention and deletion

Your entries remain on your device until you change or erase them. Use Settings → Erase all my data to remove the app's saved trip, personal entries, and preferences from that device. Deleting the app also removes its local app container. Manage exported copies and operating-system backups separately; erasing local data does not erase those copies.

Support and this website

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical connection information under its privacy statement. The site does not add analytics, advertising, forms, third-party fonts, or tracking scripts.

If you open a GitHub support issue, the content you submit is public and is handled by GitHub. Do not post backups, health information, booking details, or private contact information.

Children

Tabi does not require an account or ask for a date of birth. Personal entries remain on the device unless the user chooses to share them. The app is a general travel-planning tool, not a service designed specifically for children.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app's handling of information changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. For a privacy question or concern, contact Tabi support with a general description that does not disclose personal information.
